Who We Are

Two guys from Chennai with one obsession

Prakash and Kaviarasu didn't set out to
open a shop. They set out to find a bun
worth eating. When they couldn't find one,
they built Bun Fit.

From the pillow-soft Butter Bun to the
jet-black Oreo Bun — white chocolate spilling

the menu is deliberate, bold, and Chennai-made.

No shortcuts. No compromises.

The Journey

Our Story

01

A Hunger for Something Real

It started with a simple question: why is every bun in Chennai so ordinary? Prakash and Kaviarasu decided to answer it themselves — with a shop that takes the humble bun as seriously as any fine dining ingredient.

02

Charcoal, Chocolate & Obsession

One experiment led to another. Jet-black charcoal domes. White chocolate that overflows. Dark choco that drips like lava. Boost fillings that taste like childhood. Every new bun pushed further than the last.

03

Chennai Found Them

They didn't go chasing the city. The city came to them. Word spread one bite at a time — people photographing their Oreo Buns before eating them, texting friends about the Boost Bun, coming back every single day.
