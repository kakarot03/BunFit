Our Story

Two guys. One obsession. Six buns.

"Redefining the humble bun, one bold filling at a time."

It started the way most great things do — with hunger, stubbornness, and an unwillingness to settle for ordinary. Prakash and Kaviarasu weren't looking to open a café. They were looking for a bun worth eating. When they couldn't find one, they made it themselves.

Bun Fit was born in Chennai — a city that knows its food and forgives nothing. The classic Butter Bun was the beginning: simple, pillowy, honest. But Prakash's mind doesn't stay still for long. One experiment led to another, and soon the menu had charcoal buns filled with white chocolate that overflowed onto your fingers, dark chocolate creations so glossy they looked sculpted, and nostalgia-loaded Boost and Horlicks fillings that made grown adults reach for their phones to text their moms.

Kaviarasu made sure every bun that left the shop was worth the wait. No shortcuts. No compromises. Just two guys from Chennai who decided that the humble bun deserved better — and proved it one order at a time.
